# Zevoir Technologies — HR Guidelines

## 1. Working Hours
Standard working hours are **9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday**, with a 1-hour lunch break. Core collaboration hours are 10:30 AM – 4:30 PM, during which employees are expected to be available.

## 2. Probation Period
All new hires undergo a probation period of **3 months** from their date of joining. Confirmation is based on a performance review conducted by the reporting manager.

## 3. Attendance
Employees must log attendance via the HR portal daily. Repeated unexplained absences or persistent late logins may result in a formal HR review.

## 4. Communication Channels
- Official communication and approvals: company email.
- Day-to-day team communication: Slack/WhatsApp as designated by each team.
- Formal HR requests (leave, reimbursement, grievance): HR portal only.

## 5. Grievance Redressal
Employees may raise workplace concerns confidentially with HR via the grievance email or in-person request for a meeting. HR commits to acknowledging any grievance within 2 working days.

## 6. Code of Conduct
Employees are expected to maintain professionalism, punctuality, and confidentiality regarding client and company data at all times. Breach of confidentiality is treated as a serious disciplinary matter.

## 7. Performance Reviews
Formal performance reviews are conducted **twice a year** (mid-year and annual), with ratings feeding into compensation revisions and promotion decisions.

## 8. Reimbursements
Approved business expenses (client travel, approved training) are reimbursed within 15 working days of submitting receipts through the HR portal.
