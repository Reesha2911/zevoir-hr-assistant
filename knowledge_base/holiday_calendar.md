## Notes
- Public holidays are paid and apply to all employees regardless of leave balance.
- If a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday is observed as a substitute holiday.
- Employees required to work on a public holiday are entitled to a compensatory day off within the following month, subject to manager approval.
- This calendar applies to Zevoir Technologies' Australian offices only.
