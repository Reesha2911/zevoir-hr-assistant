# Zevoir Technologies — Leave Policy

## 1. Casual Leave
All confirmed employees are entitled to **12 casual leaves per calendar year**, credited at the rate of 1 per month. Casual leave must be applied for at least 1 day in advance via the HR portal, except in genuine emergencies. Casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next year and lapses on 31 December.

## 2. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to **8 sick leaves per calendar year**. For sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days, a medical certificate must be submitted to HR within 3 working days of returning to work. Sick leave cannot be combined with casual leave in the same request without HR approval.

## 3. Earned/Annual Leave
Confirmed employees accrue **1.5 earned leave days per month** (18 days per year), credited at the start of each quarter. Earned leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days. Earned leave requests must be submitted at least 7 days in advance and require manager approval.

## 4. Leave Request Process
1. Log the leave request on the HR portal or notify your manager directly for casual leave under 2 days.
2. Manager approval is required for all leave types except single-day casual leave taken with prior notice.
3. Leave balances are visible on the HR portal at all times.
4. Unapproved absences will be marked as Leave Without Pay (LWP).

## 5. Public Holidays
Public holidays are governed separately by the Zevoir Technologies Holiday Calendar document and are not deducted from any leave balance.

## 6. Maternity & Paternity Leave
Maternity leave: **12 weeks** paid leave for confirmed female employees, as per statutory guidelines.
Paternity leave: **5 working days** paid leave for confirmed male employees, to be availed within 1 month of the child's birth.
