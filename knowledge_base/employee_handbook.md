# Zevoir Technologies — Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

## 1. Welcome
This handbook outlines key policies and expectations for all Zevoir Technologies employees. It should be read alongside the Leave Policy, Work From Home Policy, Holiday Calendar, and HR Guidelines documents.

## 2. Onboarding
New employees receive a laptop, company email account, and access credentials on their first day. IT orientation and HR onboarding sessions are scheduled within the first week of joining.

## 3. Reporting Structure
Each employee reports to a designated manager who is responsible for approving leave requests, WFH requests, and conducting performance reviews.

## 4. Notice Period
The standard notice period for resignation is **30 days** for confirmed employees and **15 days** for employees on probation.

## 5. Benefits
- Health insurance coverage for the employee (family coverage available at employee's cost).
- Access to an Employee Assistance Program (EAP) for confidential counselling support.
- Annual learning and development budget, subject to manager approval.

## 6. Equal Opportunity
Zevoir Technologies is an equal opportunity employer and does not tolerate discrimination or harassment of any kind. Any concerns should be raised through the HR grievance process outlined in the HR Guidelines.

## 7. Confidentiality & Data Protection
All employees must adhere to client confidentiality agreements and internal data protection standards. Sharing client or company data outside authorized channels is strictly prohibited.

## 8. Exit Process
Upon resignation, employees must complete a knowledge transfer, return company assets (laptop, access cards), and complete an exit interview with HR before their last working day.
