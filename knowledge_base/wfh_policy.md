# Zevoir Technologies — Work From Home (WFH) Policy

## 1. Eligibility
All confirmed employees who have completed their probation period are eligible to request Work From Home. Interns and employees on probation require explicit manager approval for any WFH request.

## 2. Standard WFH Allowance
Employees may work from home up to **4 days per month**, subject to manager approval and no critical in-office meetings scheduled that day.

## 3. Ad-Hoc / Situational WFH
For situational reasons such as feeling unwell, minor personal emergencies, or public transport disruptions, employees may request a single-day WFH by informing their manager as early as possible on the same day, ideally before 9:30 AM. This does not require submission through the HR portal but should be confirmed in writing (email or chat) to the manager.

## 4. Notification Requirement
All WFH requests, whether planned or ad-hoc, must be communicated to the direct manager in advance or as early as possible on the day of WFH, along with expected availability during work hours.

## 5. Work Expectations While on WFH
- Employees must be reachable during standard working hours (9:30 AM – 6:30 PM).
- Employees must attend all scheduled meetings virtually unless otherwise excused.
- Deliverables and deadlines remain unchanged while working from home.

## 6. Location Restrictions
Work From Home requests are approved only for locations within Australia. Any request to work remotely from outside the country for an extended period (e.g., "workation" arrangements) must be raised separately with HR at least 4 weeks in advance and is evaluated on a case-by-case basis; it is **not covered under the standard WFH policy**.

## 7. Equipment
Employees working from home are responsible for maintaining a stable internet connection. Zevoir Technologies does not currently provide a home office equipment allowance.
